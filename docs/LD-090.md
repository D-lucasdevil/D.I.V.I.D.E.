# **LD-090 — “Forever Diamonds”**    

**Designation:** Anomalous Artifact / Ring    
**Threat Level:** **Class IV — Tier 4**    
**Containment Status:** Contained    
**Origin:** Unknown    
**Current Location:** D.I.V.I.D.E. High-Security Artifact Storage     

LD-090 was recovered from a jewelry store in Canada by Special Operations Unit **J3αω**, designated *“Jewel Divers.”*     

The shopkeeper claimed that the ring had simply appeared among the store's inventory one morning. No purchase order, manufacturer information, or supplier record corresponding to LD-090 could be located.    

LD-090 resembles a conventional marital ring. The band appears to consist of an anomalous platinum-like alloy, with a **0.1-carat transparent pink diamond** embedded into its center.     

Despite possessing the physical characteristics of a diamond, the gemstone is classified as a form of **Spirit Stone**, displaying anomalous reactions to emotions associated with love, passion, attachment, and desire.     

The initials **ΕΤΔ** are engraved on the interior of the band.     
 
---    

## **Primary Ability — Equivalent Exchange**    

Equivalent Exchange is automatically activated whenever the wearer attempts to utilize one of LD-090's anomalous abilities.    

Before the ability activates, LD-090 requires the user to surrender a measurable quantity of something possessing **personal or functional value**.    

The exchange must be detrimental to the user.   

Examples include:    

* Remaining lifespan    
* Physical stamina   
* Blood volume    
* Memory    
* Monetary currency    
* Sleep     
* Sensory ability     
* Temporary physical capabilities     

The ring **cannot accept completely meaningless sacrifices**.    

For example, sacrificing a random pebble produces no effect.    

The greater the requested effect, the greater the required exchange.    

### **Important limitation**    

The user **cannot directly sacrifice their entire lifespan, soul, or other permanent vital component in exchange for an arbitrarily powerful effect.**     

LD-090 automatically establishes a maximum exchange threshold.     

A single activation cannot reduce the user's estimated lifespan by more than **30 days**, regardless of the desired ability.    

Multiple activations can still accumulate this cost.    

This prevents the user from simply saying:     

> *"I sacrifice 80 years of my life and become invincible."*    

The ring will reject the exchange.

# **Ability I — Reality Manipulation**    

The wearer can establish temporary **Rules** affecting themselves or their immediate surroundings.    

### **Maximum: 2 Rules simultaneously.**    

Rules must be:    

* physically possible within the anomalous framework of LD-090     
* relatively simple    
* temporary    
* limited to the immediate area    

Examples:    

> **"My body cannot catch fire for 30 seconds."**   

> **"I can move at 150 km/h for 10 seconds."**    

> **"The floor beneath me becomes extremely difficult to break."**    

The ring **cannot create matter, resurrect people, erase anomalies, stop time, rewrite history, or directly command another anomaly to die.**    

Rules affecting another person require substantially more Equivalent Exchange than rules affecting the wearer.    
  
### Duration     

Rules last between **5 and 60 seconds**, depending on their complexity.    

Afterward, the rule disappears automatically.    

Attempting to establish a third rule immediately cancels the oldest active rule.    

---   

# **Ability II — Promised Obliteration**     

LD-090 can emit a concentrated pink energy beam from its gemstone.    

The beam now has:   

**Diameter:** ~1.2 meters    
**Maximum range:** ~80 meters    
**Maximum continuous duration:** 3 seconds    

The beam possesses tremendous destructive power and can:      

* melt reinforced steel    
* destroy concrete structures    
* severely damage anomalous entities    
* penetrate most conventional armor    

However, it **cannot simply erase matter**.    

Against high-tier anomalies, the beam becomes progressively less effective.    

A Tier 4 anomaly can potentially survive a direct hit.    

The beam also requires a **large Equivalent Exchange cost**, preventing repeated firing.    

---     

# **Ability III — Splinter Cell**    

The user may designate a non-living object weighing up to **15 kg**.    

LD-090 surrounds the object with spiritual energy before the command:     

> **"Splinter Cell."**    
 
The object then breaks apart into dozens or hundreds of sharp fragments.    

The user can designate **one direction** for the resulting fragments.    
 
Once fired, their trajectory **cannot be changed**.    

The fragments cannot intentionally strike the wearer and will automatically pass around them.    

### Limitations     

The ability cannot:    

* affect living targets directly    
* split anomalous entities    
* affect objects larger than the mass limit    
* redirect the fragments after firing    
* create additional material    

The fragments retain their original physical composition.    

For example, splintering a steel object creates **steel fragments**, not magical projectiles.    

 
---    
 
# **Spirit Stone — Emotional Amplification**     

LD-090 becomes more powerful when surrounded by intense emotions associated with:    

**love, devotion, desire, desperation, or obsession.**    

However, this does **not** directly increase its destructive power.    

Instead, emotional intensity slightly reduces the Equivalent Exchange cost.    

This creates the creepy implication that the ring may actually **encourage emotional attachment** between its wearer and other people.    

Whether this is intentional remains unknown.    

---    

# **Containment**    

LD-090 must remain inside a sealed artifact chamber whenever not undergoing authorized testing.    

The ring is kept inside a transparent armored case.    

No individual may wear LD-090 without authorization from a **DIVIDE Level 5 researcher or higher**.    

Any researcher attempting to activate LD-090 must have their:     

* estimated lifespan   
* physical condition    
* financial assets    
* medical records    
* psychological profile    

recorded beforehand.    

This is necessary because Equivalent Exchange has demonstrated the ability to consume resources that researchers initially considered insignificant.
