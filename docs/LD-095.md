# LD-095 — “Scythe of Sync”    

**Designation:** Anomalous Weapon / Synchronized Artifact     
**Threat Level:** **Class III — Tier 3**    
**Clearance Required:** DIVIDE Level 3+ or to be provided in case of another anomaly breach conditionally    
**Containment Status:** Contained    
**Current Location:** Secured alongside LD-047    
**Current Status:** In Tune With LD-047    

---    

## Overview    

LD-095, designated **“Scythe of Sync,”** is an anomalous scythe constructed from an unknown gold, black, and grey metallic material.    

The weapon appears to contain a fragment of the anomalous capabilities belonging to **LD-047**.    

The exact method by which this fragment was transferred into LD-095 remains unknown.    

The following inscription is engraved along the weapon's handle:    

> **“To be in tune is to be in sync.”**    

Despite its appearance, LD-095 possesses no detectable conventional power source.    

---    
 
# Synchronization    

LD-095 cannot be properly utilized by an ordinary individual.    

Upon LD-047, or an individual specifically trusted by LD-047, making physical contact with the weapon, a translucent display appears within the user's field of vision.    

The display produces no detectable radiation and does not appear to physically interact with the user's eyes.    

The interface displays information regarding LD-095's currently available abilities.    

Researchers have designated this process **Synchronization.**    

Individuals without sufficient synchronization can still physically wield LD-095, but the weapon behaves similarly to an extremely durable conventional scythe and provides access to none of its anomalous functions.    

---    
 
# Primary Ability — Manifestation    

LD-095 can generate firearms directly from anomalous energy.    

The weapon can initially produce:   

* Pistols     
* Revolvers         
* SMGs          
* Shotguns       
* Machine pistols       

The generated weapons require no conventional ammunition.        

Instead, LD-095 produces ammunition internally.            

The ammunition adapts to the weapon being manifested.       

A single manifestation can produce approximately **30–150 rounds**, depending on the weapon.      

Once depleted, the weapon disappears.        

The ammunition cannot be removed from the manifested weapon and stored for later use.          

---        

# Secondary Ability — Separation          

LD-095 can separate itself into two independent weapons.         

The scythe divides into:        

### **The Staff**           

A reinforced anomalous bo staff capable of surviving impacts that would destroy conventional metal weapons.             

The staff can channel a portion of LD-095's anomalous energy into strikes.           

A sufficiently powerful hit can:           

* Knock back armored targets          
* Break concrete            
* Crack reinforced doors           
* Disrupt weaker anomalies           

### **The Armblade**            

The curved blade of LD-095 can detach and form an anomalous arm-mounted blade.          

The Armblade possesses considerably greater cutting power than the original scythe edge and can penetrate most conventional body armor.          

It is capable of cutting through steel and reinforced concrete with sufficient force.         

However, it cannot freely cut through higher-tier anomalous defenses.         

The two weapons can be recombined instantly.         

---        
 
# Sync — Combat Resonance         

The primary reason LD-095 qualifies as a Tier 3 anomaly is its ability to synchronize with its wielder during combat.         

Every successful action performed with LD-095 gradually builds **Sync.**         

Sync can be increased through:         

* Successfully striking a target          
* Blocking an incoming attack          
* Successfully dodging an attack           
* Switching between manifested weapons         
* Maintaining a continuous combat rhythm           
* Successfully defeating hostile targets         

The weapon becomes progressively more responsive as Sync increases.        

At low Sync, LD-095 behaves similarly to an advanced anomalous weapon.         

At high Sync, the wielder begins moving with the weapon almost instinctively.          

Their reaction speed, coordination, balance, timing and weapon handling increase dramatically.          

The user does not become physically superhuman.                   
 
Instead, LD-095 appears to eliminate unnecessary movement and continuously correct the wielder's actions.       

Experienced users have described the effect as:          

> **“Fighting before you realize you've moved.”**            

---          

# Sync Level II — Armament            

Once sufficient Sync has been accumulated, LD-095 unlocks its second manifestation level.          

The firearm selection expands to include:        

* Assault rifles           
* Battle rifles            
* Designated marksman rifles           
* Sniper rifles           
* Light machine guns         
* Specialized anomalous firearms           

At this stage, some manifested weapons begin displaying minor anomalous properties.               

Observed examples include:           

**Penetration Rounds** — capable of passing through several layers of conventional armor.          

**Impact Rounds** — release a short-range kinetic shockwave upon striking a surface.           
 
**Tracking Rounds** — subtly correct their trajectory toward the designated target.                   
 
**Disruption Rounds** — interfere with weaker anomalous abilities upon impact.         
 
The user can only maintain **one anomalous firearm manifestation at a time.**              

---         

# Sync Level III — Overdrive         

At maximum Sync, LD-095 enters a temporary state designated **Overdrive.**          

During Overdrive, the weapon begins emitting a faint golden-black energy.           

The wielder gains:        

* Extremely enhanced reaction speed         
* Near-perfect weapon coordination          
* Increased movement speed          
* Greater resistance to physical trauma         
* Ability to instantly switch between the scythe, staff, armblade and manifested firearms            
* Significantly increased cutting power           
* Reduced physical exhaustion while using LD-095              

The weapon's combat efficiency reaches its maximum during this state.         

Every successful melee strike can also temporarily increase the destructive capability of the next strike.         

This creates a progressively escalating combat style.         

The longer the wielder successfully fights, the more dangerous LD-095 becomes.         

However, Overdrive lasts approximately **90 seconds.**           

Once Overdrive ends, Sync immediately drops to zero.        

---        

# Perfect Tempo Hit            

While in **Overdrive**, the wielder may achieve a state of perfect tempo hit with LD-095.        

If the wielder performs a precisely timed attack at the exact moment required by the weapon, **Perfect Tempo Hit** activates.        

Upon successfully performing Perfect Tempo Hit, the **specific anomaly directly struck by the attack** is temporarily frozen for approximately **2 seconds.**          

The effect applies only to the anomaly that was directly hit.        

No surrounding anomalies, personnel, objects, or areas are affected.         

The target's physical body and anomalous activity are simultaneously halted.         

During these two seconds:         

* Active anomalous abilities stop functioning         
* Transformations temporarily freeze         
* Regeneration stops       
* Defensive anomalous effects cease functioning          
* Ongoing anomalous attacks are suspended           
* The target cannot move         

Perfect Tempo Hit does not destroy or permanently disable the affected anomaly.          

Once the two seconds have elapsed, all previously active anomalous processes resume.           

**Perfect Tempo Hit can only occur once during each Overdrive state.**          

The timing required is extremely precise.          

Repeated attempts to deliberately activate Perfect Tempo Hit have proven unreliable, with LD-095 appearing to determine independently whether the strike was performed with sufficient synchronization.            

---          
  
# Desynchronization           

LD-095's greatest weakness is its dependence on synchronization.           

Taking significant damage, losing consciousness, becoming severely distracted, or losing control of one's movements causes Sync to rapidly decrease.          

If Sync reaches zero, all enhanced abilities disappear and the weapon returns to its base scythe form.       

The weapon cannot accumulate Sync outside of active combat.          

---                   

# Physical Limitations          

Despite its anomalous capabilities, LD-095 does not make its wielder immune to injury.            

A sufficiently powerful attack can still kill or incapacitate the user.           

The weapon's enhanced defensive properties primarily improve the wielder's ability to react and survive rather than making them invulnerable.              
 
LD-095 also cannot manifest:            

* Artillery          
* Vehicles          
* Strategic weapons          
* Weapons of mass destruction           
* Unlimited quantities of ammunition          

The weapon is intended primarily for close-to-mid-range combat.          

---           

# Relationship With LD-047         

LD-095's effectiveness appears to depend partially on its relationship with LD-047.            

When used by LD-047 herself, the weapon operates at approximately **120% of its normal efficiency.**            

When used by an individual personally trusted by LD-047, the weapon operates normally.       

If an unauthorized individual attempts to use LD-095, the weapon becomes almost completely inert.         

There is currently no known method of forcibly overriding this restriction.            

---             

# Discovery — Incident 095-01           

LD-095's first confirmed manifestation occurred during a major containment breach involving **LD-047 and LD-057.**           

Multiple anomalies had escaped their containment sectors.          

Security recordings show **APA-01** walking through the damaged facility.      

The operator was carrying no visible weapon.       

Approximately several meters behind him was the severed head of a **Tier 3 anomaly** that APA-01 had apparently encountered during his movement through the facility.          

APA-01 eventually entered a heavily damaged containment sector.           

Inside, personnel discovered LD-047 in an abnormal state.          

LD-047 appeared to have become partially fused with **LD-057.**          

The exact nature of the fusion remains under investigation.              

Upon seeing APA-01, LD-047 immediately manifested LD-095.              

The weapon appeared directly in her hands.                

She extended the scythe toward APA-01.         

APA-01 stopped.           

He looked at LD-047.           

Then at the scythe.              

APA-01 stared at LD-047 for several seconds before taking the weapon.        

Witnesses later described his expression as:          

> **“A predator examining prey.”**         

APA-01 then said only:          

> **“Containment.”**           

At that exact moment, every alarm throughout the facility activated simultaneously.         

The intercom system began repeatedly announcing:            
 
> **“WARNING. CLASS IV ANOMALY LD-013 HAS BREACHED CONTAINMENT.”**         

APA-01 immediately turned toward the nearest containment corridor.        

LD-047 remained behind.        

Approximately four minutes later, all communication with the facility was lost.

# Current Assessment           

LD-095 remains classified as **Tier 3.**          

Although its capabilities are considerable, its dependence on synchronization, limited manifestation capacity, vulnerable wielder, and temporary nature of Overdrive prevent it from reaching Tier 4 classification.          

However, the presence of LD-047 significantly changes this assessment.         

When synchronized with LD-047, LD-095 demonstrates capabilities exceeding those normally expected from a Tier 3 anomalous weapon.            

Further testing has been prohibited.
