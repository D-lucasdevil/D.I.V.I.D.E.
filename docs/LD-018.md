    <div class="doc reveal"><p>This anomaly, believed to originate from the entity responsible for the “Assessment Examination” events, was a shapeshifter mimicking lost or isolated individuals.     <div class="sec reveal">Origin: The Assessment Examination</div>
    <div class="note reveal"><p>Nothing in the record explains where the Imitator came from. The only link is the one already named in the Summary: it is believed to originate from the entity responsible for the “Assessment Examination” events. Everything below is analyst speculation.</p><p>In every Assessment Examination event, personnel were separated and questioned. The questions were ordinary. The answers were checked against the file. Nobody who answered incorrectly was ever harmed. They were simply not the ones who came back.</p><p>The Imitator may be the first recovered <b>examination script</b>: a thing that gives only the answers the file expects. <b>Real people misremember. The Imitator never does.</b></p></div>

    <div class="sec reveal">Method of Operation</div>
    <ul class="list reveal"><li><b>I</b>It does not hunt. It waits for a person to become lost or isolated — cut off by a blackout, a seal, a fog, a locked ward — and then it arrives in their place.</li><li><b>II</b>It is not an intruder. It is expected. Missing personnel are searched for. When one of them comes back, no alarm sounds.</li><li><b>III</b>It replicates voice, emotion, and memory. Every authentication system in the facilities it entered relied on at least one of the three, and failed.</li><li><b>IV</b>It copies a person as they are <i>remembered</i> — by the file, by their friends, by themselves — not as they are now.</li></ul>
