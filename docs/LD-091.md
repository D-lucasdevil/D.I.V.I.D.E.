# **LD-091 — “I Love a Man in Uniform”**    

**Designation:** Anomalous Artifact / Card    
**Threat Level:** **Class III**     
**Containment Status:** Contained
**Primary Object:** Playing-card-like anomalous card      
**Associated Object:** Cold War-era U.S. military helmet     

### **Overview**     

LD-091 was recovered from a museum display containing a Cold War-era U.S. military helmet. A strange card was found wedged between the helmet and its inner lining.     

The card resembles a standard playing card, although it does not correspond to any recognized deck. Its center depicts a **frog in the process of jumping**, while the corners contain the inscription **ΕΤΔ** in place of a conventional value.    

The card itself appears ordinary until it is taken into the hand of a living person.    

Upon being claimed by a new user, visible fractures begin spreading across its surface as though the card were made of glass. These fractures remain present until the user's connection to LD-091 is severed.     

---    

## **Primary Ability — “The Muster”**    

Once claimed, LD-091 allows its owner to summon an anomalous military force.    

The size of this force is determined by the owner's history of **recruiting or killing other people**.    

The exact mechanism by which LD-091 converts these actions into soldiers remains unknown.    

The summoned personnel are fully physical, independently capable humans possessing:    
 
* Individual personalities    
* Independent thoughts    
* Military knowledge appropriate to their apparent rank    
* Standard equipment appropriate to their faction    
* Complete loyalty to the card's current owner    

The available forces appear to include personnel analogous to:    

* Army   
* Navy      
* Marines   
* Air Force    
* Other military formations not yet conclusively identified    

The uniforms, weapons and equipment vary according to the force and apparent rank of each soldier.     

There is currently **no confirmed upper limit** to the number of soldiers LD-091 can eventually accumulate.    

However, the entire accumulated force **cannot be summoned repeatedly**.     

### **Cooldown**     
  
LD-091 can only perform a full summoning **once every fourteen days**.     

Attempting to activate the card before the cooldown has expired produces no effect.    

If the owner is killed, the accumulated army immediately disappears and the card's recorded force count resets to **zero**.     

The same occurs if the card is successfully stolen and separated from its owner.     
 
Every new owner begins with **one soldier**.    

---    

# **The First Soldier**    

Every owner of LD-091 receives the same initial soldier.     

She is consistently described as a blonde woman with several visible scars across her face.     

Unlike the other summoned personnel, she has appeared with every recorded owner regardless of:    

* Country   
* Gender    
* Age    
* Military experience    
* Number of accumulated soldiers    

She is extremely quiet and emotionally restrained.    

She generally does not speak unless directly addressed and will obey the owner's commands without hesitation.    

Her behavior toward the owner is otherwise remarkably neutral.    

---    

# **Behavioral Degradation**    

The greatest danger of LD-091 is not necessarily the army itself.    

Repeated use appears to gradually alter the summoned soldiers' attitudes toward their owner.    

After approximately **5–7 full deployments**, soldiers begin developing increasingly violent and hostile impulses toward the card's owner.    

These impulses eventually become severe enough that the summoned army may attempt to **kill or sexually assault the owner**.    

The exact number of deployments required varies between users.    

The first soldier appears to be immune to this behavioral degradation.    

She will continue obeying the owner's commands unless directly ordered otherwise.    

Notably, if explicitly commanded to engage in sexual activity, she will comply, but she does not independently participate in the later hostility exhibited by the rest of the army.

### **Threat Assessment — Class III**     

LD-091 is classified as **Tier III** because its theoretical army size is potentially enormous, but its practical threat is heavily restricted by the **14-day activation interval**, the reset condition, and the increasingly unstable relationship between the owner and their summoned forces.      

A sufficiently experienced owner could nevertheless turn LD-091 into a **large-scale military threat**, particularly if they survive long enough to accumulate a substantial force.     

**Containment Directive:**    
The card is to remain in a sealed artifact chamber. Personnel are forbidden from touching LD-091 directly without **DIVIDE Level 4 authorization**.
