# LD-094 — “The Last Oath”   

Designation: Entity / Humanoid    
Threat Level: Class IV    
Clearance Required: DIVIDE Level 5+    
Containment Status: Contained

Overview:   

LD-094 takes the appearance of a young woman wearing a long black ceremonial dress. The entity's face appears completely featureless, resembling a dark silhouette despite light reflecting normally from the rest of its body. A large white ceremonial sword is usually manifested in its hands, accompanied by a floating halo above its head.   

LD-094 appears to possess a calm and unusually sympathetic personality. It rarely speaks unless directly addressed and has shown no apparent enjoyment or desire to cause unnecessary suffering.    

The primary anomalous property of LD-094 is its ability to sever connections between objects, individuals, concepts, and anomalous properties.    

Ability — “Severance”:   

LD-094 is capable of cutting the connection between two things with its manifested sword without necessarily damaging either object physically.    

Observed examples include:    

• Severing an individual's connection to a weapon, causing the individual to retain the ability to physically hold the weapon but become completely incapable of recognizing how to use it.    

• Severing an anomaly's connection to its anomalous abilities, temporarily rendering the anomaly effectively mundane.    

• Severing an individual's connection to a specific location, resulting in the individual being physically capable of entering the location while being unable to properly perceive, remember, or recognize their presence there.     

• Severing emotional connections between individuals.         

The latter is considered particularly dangerous. Subjects affected by this form of Severance retain their memories and intellectual understanding of another person while losing the emotional connection associated with them.     

For example, a subject may recognize another individual as their mother while experiencing absolutely no emotional attachment toward them.     

LD-094 has demonstrated the ability to sever anomalous connections previously believed to be impossible to separate.        

Limitation — “Three Cuts”:      

LD-094 can perform a maximum of three Severances during a single manifestation.    

After the third Severance has been performed, LD-094's sword becomes inert and the entity disappears.    

The duration of a Severance varies depending on what was affected. Effects have lasted anywhere from several seconds to multiple days.    

LD-094 has not demonstrated the ability to sever fundamental concepts such as life, existence, or consciousness. Attempts to force such a Severance result in the sword developing visible cracks before LD-094 immediately disappears.    

Secondary Ability — “The White Blade”:    

The sword manifested by LD-094 possesses extreme physical cutting capability and has successfully damaged steel, reinforced concrete, anomalous materials, conventional armor, and several known LD manifestations.    

Despite this, the sword does not possess unlimited cutting power. The more durable the target, the greater the physical effort required from LD-094.    

Defensive Ability — “Halo”:    

The halo manifested above LD-094's head functions as a defensive anomalous field.     

The field redirects incoming kinetic force away from LD-094, allowing it to deflect bullets, melee attacks, and portions of explosive force.    

The halo possesses a finite capacity and can be overloaded through sustained attacks.     

Once overloaded, the halo disappears and LD-094 becomes significantly more vulnerable to conventional weaponry.     

Discovery:     

LD-094 was discovered following reports of an anomalous phenomenon occurring within an abandoned cathedral.    

A D.I.V.I.D.E. response team entered the structure after several civilians reported seeing a woman holding a white sword beneath a glowing halo.    

Shortly after entering the structure, several members of the response team became unable to recognize one another.     

Their memories remained intact and they continued to understand that they were members of the same D.I.V.I.D.E. team. However, they no longer experienced any emotional familiarity, trust, or attachment toward one another.     

LD-094 was discovered standing in the center of the cathedral.     

Its sword had already been damaged and contained three visible cracks.     

Further investigation determined that LD-094 had not attacked the response team.    

Something else had entered the cathedral before the team arrived.    

LD-094 had apparently used all three of its Severances attempting to stop it.    

The identity and nature of the entity or object affected by these three Severances remains unknown.     

When questioned regarding the incident, LD-094 refused to provide an explanation.    

During one interrogation, a researcher asked:    

“What are you?”      

LD-094 remained silent for approximately twelve seconds before responding:    

“The thing that comes between.”     

No further explanation was provided.    

Containment Notes:    

LD-094 is not to be provoked or intentionally forced into combat.    

Personnel assigned to LD-094 are prohibited from attempting to determine what constitutes the maximum possible range of its Severance ability without authorization from DIVIDE Level 6 personnel.     

Any interaction involving LD-094's sword is to be treated as a potential anomalous event.     

Researchers are reminded that the entity's calm demeanor does not indicate that it is harmless.
