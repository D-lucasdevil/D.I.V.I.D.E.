# LD-093 — “Don’t Fear the Reaper”        

**Designation:** Weapon / Pistol         
**Threat Level:** **Tier II**         
**Clearance Required:** DIVIDE Level 3+          
**Containment Status:** Contained          
**Current Holder:** D.I.V.I.D.E. Researcher Dr. Ann Do

### Discovery         

LD-093 takes the form of a conventional pistol with a **red under-barrel assembly, black grip, and silver barrel**. The letters **ΕΤΔ** are engraved into the grip.         

LD-093 was discovered aboard a cargo ship in the possession of the ship's head of security. Following the disappearance of several crew members, a D.I.V.I.D.E. task force was dispatched to secure the weapon.         

The weapon was transported inside a secure containment case and remained untouched until controlled testing began.         
 
During the first testing procedure, subjects reported hearing an unidentified voice immediately after touching the weapon.        

The voice consistently spoke the same sentence:         
 
> **“Don't fear the reaper.”**        

No further communication has been recorded.        

---          

## Ability — “The Reaper's Touch”        

LD-093's ammunition possesses an anomalous property that affects **living targets only**.        

Rather than causing conventional ballistic damage, a successful hit causes the target to experience an immediate and severe loss of physical strength.          

The effect manifests as:       

* Sudden exhaustion.        
* Loss of muscle coordination.        
* Temporary numbness.         
* Difficulty breathing.         
* Severe dizziness.          

The effect becomes significantly weaker against larger or more durable organisms.             

A target struck repeatedly may become completely incapacitated, but **LD-093 cannot directly kill a healthy target through this ability alone**.             

The effect naturally wears off after several minutes.            

Inanimate objects are completely unaffected.              

Testing has shown that the ability is considerably less effective against high-level LDs, making LD-093 unsuitable as a primary anti-anomaly weapon.            

---          

## Sub-Ability — “We Can Be Like They Are”              

If the user mentally commands LD-093 to activate its secondary function, the **next successful shot against a living target** will trigger an anomalous transformation.           

The target will instantly dissolve into a swarm of butterflies.              

The butterflies' coloration appears to correspond to the general behavior of the target:           

* **White:** Predominantly benevolent behavior.         
* **Black:** Predominantly malicious behavior.          
* **Gray:** Predominantly neutral behavior.          
* **Mixed coloration:** Targets whose behavior cannot be easily categorized.           
 
The transformation is **not permanent**.          

The butterflies will remain active for approximately **thirty seconds** before disappearing and leaving the original target unconscious but physically unharmed.         

This ability cannot be activated more than **three times within a 24-hour period**.           

It is currently believed that LD-093 determines the coloration based upon the target's actions rather than their intentions.            

---          

## Sub-Ability — “Mando Arms”            

By pulling the pistol's barrel assembly completely backward before pulling the trigger, LD-093 activates its secondary defensive function.            

A powerful flame erupts from the barrel.              

The flame:           

* Cannot harm the weapon's user.       
* Can burn through most conventional materials.          
* Can destroy incoming physical projectiles.         
* Can melt certain metals.        
* Appears to partially disrupt anomalous energy-based attacks.         

Despite its appearance, the flame has a relatively short effective range of approximately **8 meters**.                

The ability also places considerable strain on the weapon.            

After approximately **five consecutive uses**, LD-093 becomes completely inactive for several hours.            

The ability appears primarily designed for defensive purposes rather than direct combat.           

---           

## Limitations           

LD-093 possesses several significant limitations which prevent it from being classified above Tier II.              

1. Its anomalous ammunition cannot be generated indefinitely.            
2. The weapon must be manually reloaded.           
3. The Reaper's Touch becomes progressively less effective against stronger organisms.                
4. “We Can Be Like They Are” has a strict activation limit.        
5. “Mando Arms” has a short range and lengthy recovery period.           
6. The weapon itself can be physically damaged.            
7. LD-093 has demonstrated no ability to repair itself.           
8. The weapon provides no enhanced physical abilities to its user.            

Despite its anomalous properties, LD-093 remains fundamentally a **specialized firearm rather than a high-level anomaly**.            

---            

## Current Status           

LD-093 is currently in the possession of **D.I.V.I.D.E. Researcher Dr. Ann Do**.               

Testing has shown that Dr. Ann Do demonstrates unusually high compatibility with the weapon, allowing her to activate its abilities with significantly less preparation than other tested personnel.              

Use of LD-093 outside controlled testing is prohibited unless an extreme circumstance warrants deployment.           

D.I.V.I.D.E. currently considers LD-093 a useful **containment and utility weapon**, rather than an anti-LD weapon.
