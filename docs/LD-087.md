# File: LD-087 — "Second Skin"    

**Designation:** Parasitic Anomalous Organism    

**Threat Level:** Class II   

**Clearance Required:** DIVIDE Level 4+   

**Containment Status:** Active Containment    



![Doro Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HOE6V-vX0AAVZ6i?format=jpg&name=medium)


---   

## Overview    

LD-087, nicknamed **"Second Skin,"** resembles a translucent restraint suit measuring approximately **190 cm** in height. The material is elastic, semi-organic, and displays muscle-like fibers beneath its transparent surface.    

Despite having no visible skeleton or nervous system, LD-087 is capable of independent movement. It crawls using all four limbs before suddenly launching itself onto nearby living organisms.     

Once attached, removal becomes extremely difficult.     

---     

## Infection Process      

Upon making contact with a host, LD-087 contracts around the victim's body within seconds.     

The suit seals around every opening while remaining transparent enough for observers to see the person trapped inside.    

The victim remains fully conscious.    
 
Internal scans indicate that thousands of microscopic tendrils extend through the suit and anchor themselves into the outer layers of the host's nervous system.     

Within several minutes, voluntary movement is almost completely lost.    

---    

## Behavior    

LD-087 controls the host's body with unnerving precision.    

Witnesses often mistake infected individuals for healthy personnel until prolonged observation reveals abnormal posture, delayed speech, and unnatural movement patterns.    

The organism actively seeks isolated areas before searching for additional victims.    

---     

## Biological Process     

The parasite slowly consumes nutrients directly from the host.    

Rather than killing immediately, LD-087 intentionally prolongs survival.    

The suit regulates oxygen intake and bodily functions just enough to keep the host alive while minimizing energy expenditure.     

Complete consumption of an average adult requires approximately **10–14 days**.     
 
Throughout the process, the victim generally remains conscious.    

---     
 
## Weaknesses    

Despite its terrifying method of attack, LD-087 is physically fragile.    

Recommended response:   

* Immediate use of incendiary weapons.   
* High-caliber firearms.   
* Industrial cutting tools.   
* Sustained electrical discharge.    

If destroyed during the early stages of infection, the host has a reasonable chance of survival.    

After several days, extensive nervous system damage makes recovery unlikely.    

---    

## Containment    

LD-087 is stored inside a negative-pressure containment chamber with automated suppression systems.    

The chamber contains no living organisms.    

Personnel are prohibited from entering alone.    

If the entity becomes mobile, the chamber is immediately flooded with superheated steam before response teams enter.    

---    
 
## Incident Log 087-03     

A maintenance worker mistakenly entered the containment chamber after an access control malfunction.    

Security footage showed LD-087 remaining completely motionless for nearly four minutes before abruptly leaping onto the individual.     

The victim remained standing throughout the initial infection, repeatedly stating:    

> "I can't move."     

Containment personnel neutralized the organism approximately ninety seconds later.    

The victim survived but suffered permanent paralysis below the neck.     

(PS: The victim is in extreme pain the moment they are cought and sealed inside LD-087 they are also being consumed alive thru out the process of 14 days)   


---   

## Threat Assessment    

Although only designated **Class II**, LD-087 has one of the highest successful ambush rates among lower-tier anomalies.   

Researchers consistently describe the entity as disturbing not because of its physical strength, but because victims remain visibly alive throughout most of the infection process.
