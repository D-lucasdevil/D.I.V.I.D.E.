# LD-092 — “THE LAST WANDERER”   

**Designation:** LD-092   
**Classification:** Humanoid / Extraterrestrial    
**Threat Level:** **REDACTED**    
**Clearance Required:** DIVIDE Level 7+    
**Containment Status:** **UNCONTAINED — NON-HOSTILE**

## Overview    

LD-092 is a humanoid entity of unknown origin possessing an anomalous level of destructive capability.    

Despite possessing enough power to potentially destroy an entire planetary body, LD-092 demonstrates little interest in conquest, territorial expansion, or interaction with populated worlds.    

The entity instead travels between planets aboard an unidentified spacecraft, generally avoiding heavily populated planets entirely.    

LD-092 has repeatedly demonstrated an apparent desire to **avoid using its anomalous abilities**.     

Its preferred method of combat consists of conventional weaponry, including firearms, blades, explosives, and other manually operated weapons.     

The entity appears to possess extensive combat experience despite its otherwise unknown origin.     

---    

## Physical Description       

LD-092 possesses the physical appearance of a young adult humanoid female.      

The entity's body is remarkably fragile.     

Medical observation following limited encounters suggests that LD-092 possesses **much less physical durability than an average human being**.     

Minor injuries that would be considered negligible to most anomalous entities can cause significant damage to LD-092.    

This extreme physical fragility appears to exist in direct opposition to the destructive capability of its anomalous abilities.     

LD-092 generally wears a highly advanced armored suit of unknown manufacture.     

The armor appears to provide protection against environmental hazards rather than significantly increasing the entity's physical durability.     

The suit possesses an integrated helmet capable of automatically deploying when LD-092 enters a vacuum.     

---     

## Behavior    

LD-092 is generally non-aggressive.      

The entity appears to deliberately avoid populated areas whenever possible.     

Upon entering a planetary system, LD-092 will generally:     

1. Locate a planet or celestial body with sufficient resources.     
2. Avoid major population centers.     
3. Resupply its spacecraft.     
4. Remain for the minimum amount of time necessary.     
5. Depart the system.     

The entity has never been observed attempting to establish territory.     

It has also never demonstrated interest in political structures, governments, military organizations, or planetary resources beyond those necessary for survival and continued travel.     

When confronted, LD-092 generally attempts to flee.     

When escape is impossible, the entity will resort to conventional weapons.     

LD-092 appears to understand the destructive potential of its anomalous abilities and actively avoids using them.     

---    

# Anomalous Properties    

## I — Conventional Combat Proficiency    

Despite its physical fragility, LD-092 demonstrates extraordinary proficiency with conventional weaponry.    

Observed weapons include:     

* Swords    
* Pistols     
* Rifles     
* Heavy firearms    
* Explosives    
* Unknown energy weapons      
* Improvised weapons     

LD-092 appears capable of rapidly adapting to unfamiliar weapons.      

The entity has also demonstrated exceptional spatial awareness and reaction speed during combat.     

It is currently unknown whether these abilities are anomalous or simply the result of extensive training.     

---     

## II — Unknown Energy Manipulation     

LD-092 possesses an anomalous energy-generating capability.     

The energy generally manifests as an intense **blue luminous phenomenon** originating from the entity's body.      

The exact nature of this energy remains unknown.      

Attempts to measure it have been unsuccessful.       

The energy does not appear to conform to known forms of electromagnetic, thermal, nuclear, or gravitational energy.     

LD-092 appears capable of manipulating this energy through physical movements.       

The entity has been observed raising one arm immediately before activating the phenomenon.       

---     

## III — Planetary Destruction     

Only **one confirmed activation** of LD-092's anomalous power has been recorded.     

The event occurred approximately **26 light-years from the nearest DIVIDE observation facility**.     

A planetary body was observed experiencing catastrophic structural failure immediately after LD-092 activated its anomalous ability.     

The available recording shows LD-092 raising one arm.      

A blue light subsequently manifested around the entity.      

Within seconds, the planet began to violently fracture.     

The planetary body subsequently separated into numerous massive fragments.     

No conventional explosion was detected.     

The planet appeared to have been **forcibly torn apart**.        

The total energy involved could not be accurately calculated.       

DIVIDE analysts have consequently classified LD-092's potential destructive capacity as:      

> **UNKNOWN.**        

The possibility that planetary destruction represents the upper limit of LD-092's abilities has been rejected.      

There is currently no evidence establishing an upper limit.        

---      

## IV — Power Instability      

LD-092's greatest anomalous property may simultaneously represent its greatest weakness.      

The entity appears unable to perfectly control the amount of power it produces.      

Observations suggest that increasing the output of its anomalous abilities increases the risk of catastrophic collateral damage.      

The entity therefore appears to intentionally suppress its abilities.     

This may explain its preference for conventional weapons.       

Whether LD-092 is incapable of safely controlling its power or simply fears the consequences of doing so remains unknown.

# Recorded Planetary Incident     

**Incident Designation:** LD-092-01   
**Distance from Earth:** Approximately 26 light-years     

DIVIDE monitoring equipment detected an unidentified spacecraft entering a planetary system.     

LD-092 exited the spacecraft and entered the atmosphere of an unconfirmed planetary body.          

The reason for the entity's presence remains unknown.         

Shortly afterward, an unidentified hostile force engaged LD-092.       

The available recording becomes heavily distorted during the encounter.         

LD-092 is eventually observed standing motionless.         

The entity raises its right arm.         

A blue luminous phenomenon appears around the arm.         

Approximately three seconds later, the planet begins violently fracturing.     

Large sections of the planetary crust separate from the planetary body.      

Atmospheric disturbances become severe enough to interfere with the recording.      

The planet ultimately breaks apart.     

LD-092 remains alive.    

The entity's spacecraft subsequently approaches its location.     

The spacecraft's entrance mechanism activates automatically.     

LD-092 enters the vessel.      

The spacecraft then departs the system.     

No further anomalous activity was recorded.

# The Spacecraft          

LD-092 possesses an unidentified spacecraft.           

The vessel appears capable of:           

* Interplanetary travel        
* Interstellar travel         
* Atmospheric entry         
* Atmospheric flight           
* Operation within vacuum          
* Automated environmental protection         
* Automatic helmet deployment          
* Autonomous navigation                

The spacecraft has demonstrated the ability to locate LD-092 independently.          

During Incident LD-092-01, the spacecraft was observed traveling directly toward LD-092 after the planetary destruction event.        

The vessel appeared to recognize the entity without any visible communication.         

DIVIDE has therefore proposed that the spacecraft possesses either:           

**A)** an advanced autonomous intelligence,           

**B)** a biological connection to LD-092,        

**C)** a remote connection to LD-092,           

or         

**D)** an unknown anomalous mechanism.           

None of these theories have been confirmed.            

---         

# Containment          

**Active containment is currently considered impossible.**           

LD-092 is not currently hostile toward DIVIDE and has never attempted to approach Earth.         

Because the entity demonstrates an apparent desire to avoid populated worlds, DIVIDE has adopted a policy of **non-interference**.         

Personnel are forbidden from attempting to intercept LD-092 unless the entity demonstrates hostile intent toward Earth or another protected population.      

Under no circumstances is an attempt to force LD-092 into combat authorized without direct approval from DIVIDE Level 7 personnel.       

The reasoning is simple:        

**LD-092 does not need to be physically powerful to be catastrophic.**       

If the entity's planetary destruction capability is repeatable, provoking LD-092 could result in the loss of the human civilization and humankind all together.    
 
---         

# Weaknesses        

Despite its REDACTED classification, LD-092 possesses an extreme physical vulnerability.        

The entity can be injured by conventional weapons.        

It is currently believed that sufficiently powerful conventional weaponry could potentially kill LD-092 before it activates its anomalous abilities.         

However, this possibility has never been tested.        

No DIVIDE researcher has been authorized to conduct such an experiment.          

The entity's spacecraft may also provide additional protection or emergency support.          

It is currently unknown whether LD-092 can survive the destruction of its spacecraft.            

---          

# Threat Assessment           

LD-092 is classified as **REDACTED** rather than Tier IV or Tier V.        

This classification is not based on its physical combat capability.          

In direct physical combat, LD-092 may actually be considerably weaker than many Tier III anomalies.          

The classification exists because:             

> **The maximum destructive potential of LD-092 cannot currently be determined.**         

A Tier IV anomaly may be capable of destroying buildings, cities, facilities, or even entire regions.        

LD-092 has already demonstrated the ability to destroy a **planet**.            

If the observed event represents only a fraction of the entity's actual power, its theoretical destructive capability could exceed every currently documented anomaly except the highest-order entities.

# Research Notes          
 
LD-092 has not willingly communicated with DIVIDE.       

Attempts to establish communication have resulted in the entity simply leaving the area.          

No known language has been identified.         

Its spacecraft's technology does not correspond to any known civilization currently catalogued by DIVIDE.         

The entity's origin remains unknown.           

It is also unknown whether LD-092 is:          

* Human        
* Artificial            
* Extraterrestrial           
* An anomalous construct           
* A survivor of an unknown civilization            
* Or something else entirely.
