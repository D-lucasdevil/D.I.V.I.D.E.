    <!-- OVERVIEW -->
    <div class="section-label">// Overview //</div>
    <div class="content-block">
      <p>LD-089 is an anomalous entity capable of existing within a single human host at any given time. Its dormant manifestation initially appears as a missing-person poster — found near the bodies of 20 D.I.V.I.D.E. personnel and one civilian.</p>
      <p>The poster displays a photograph of what observers have described as an "old friend" bearing a deep grin. The clarity of that photograph depends entirely on the observer. Those who have not met the requirement to become a host see only blur and distortion. Those who have reached their requirement see the face with increasing clarity. No two observers necessarily see the same individual.</p>
    </div>

    <!-- THE HOST -->
    <div class="section-label">// The Host //</div>
    <div class="content-block">
      <p>LD-089 can inhabit only one living human at a time. The entity does not seize control unless the host is deemed no longer worthy or dies. Instead it establishes a form of mutual existence — the host retains complete control and can communicate with LD-089 internally through thoughts, dreams, reflections, and occasionally through the posters themselves.</p>
    </div>

    </div>

    <!-- MANIFESTATION -->
    <div class="section-label">// The Manifestation State //</div>
    <div class="content-block">
      <p>The host can voluntarily activate LD-089. Upon activation the host's body becomes surrounded by anomalous paper-like material. The transformation is instantaneous. Two enormous wings emerge from the host's back — composed entirely of overlapping missing-person posters, each containing a different photograph. None of the faces can ever be properly identified even at extreme close range.</p>
      <p>The wings appear to possess immense structural strength despite being composed of ordinary paper-like material. When damaged they simply shed individual posters and reconstruct. Burning a poster does not destroy LD-089. The destroyed photograph simply reappears elsewhere within the wings.</p>
    </div>

Despite its appearance, the blade reaches temperatures capable of producing intense thermal damage on contact. The heat does not behave like conventional fire. It appears to originate from the information contained within the paper itself. Anything struck briefly displays the appearance of burning paper before the effect disappears. The host can dismiss and recreate it at will.</div>

Researchers initially classified the host as effectively invulnerable. This assessment was later proven incorrect.</p>
