# **LD-089 — "The Missing"**   

**Designation: Anomalous Parasite / Memetic Entity**    
**Threat Level: Class III**   
**Clearance Required: DIVIDE Level 4+**    
**Containment Status: Contained — Host Dependent**   

# Overview   

LD-089 is an anomalous entity capable of existing within a single human host at any given time.   

Its dormant manifestation initially appears to be a missing-person poster.    

The poster was originally found near the dead bodies of 20 D.I.V.I.D.E. Investigation and containment team members a along with a civilian    
 
The poster displays a missing poster with a photograph of what has been described as a photo of a ‘old friend’ with a deep grin.    
 
However, the clarity of that photograph depends entirely upon the observer.     

For individuals who have not met the requirement to be a host of LD-089, the face will appear blurred, distorted, redacted, or otherwise impossible to identify.    

Those who have reached their requirement to become a host of LD-089 report that the photograph becomes increasingly clear.    

Individuals who have experienced all known manifestations are capable of seeing the face perfectly.    

No two observers necessarily see the same individual.    

The Host    

LD-089 can inhabit only one living human at a time.    

The entity does not take control of its host until the host is deemed no longer worthy or has died.    

Instead of seizing control, it establishes a form of mutual existence.    

The host retains complete control of their body and can communicate with LD-089 internally.    

The entity appears capable of communicating through thoughts, dreams, reflections, and occasionally through the missing posters themselves.     

Hosts have described LD-089 as:    

"Someone that knows more than they decide to let on."    

As well as stating    

“Their voice is incredibly calm for being on such a flimsy boat in the roaring waves.”    

**The Manifestation State**  

The host can voluntarily activate LD-089.   

Upon activation, the host's body becomes surrounded by anomalous paper-like material.   

The transformation is instantaneous.   

Two enormous wings emerge from the host's back.    

The wings are composed entirely of overlapping missing-person posters.   

Every poster contains a different photograph.   

None of the faces can ever be properly identified.   

Even photographs viewed at extremely close range become distorted whenever an observer attempts to determine the identity of the person depicted.    

The wings appear to possess immense structural strength despite being composed of ordinary paper-like material.   

**Anomalous Wings**   

The wings provide the host with:   

Flight.    
Extreme maneuverability.    
Protection from conventional projectiles.    
Limited regeneration.    
The ability to generate additional paper structures.   
The ability to create temporary duplicates of individual posters.   
Resistance to most conventional physical damage.    

When damaged, the wings simply shed individual posters and reconstruct themselves.    

Burning a poster does not destroy LD-089.    

The destroyed photograph simply reappears somewhere else within the wings.   

**The Blade**     

LD-089 can generate a single anomalous weapon known internally as the Paperbrand.    

The weapon resembles a long, impossibly thin sword constructed from compressed layers of paper.    

Despite its appearance, the blade reaches temperatures capable of producing intense thermal damage upon contact.    

It does not behave like conventional fire.    

The heat appears to originate from the information contained within the paper itself.    

Anything struck by the blade briefly displays the appearance of burning paper before the effect disappears.     

The host can dismiss and recreate the weapon at will.    
  
**Defensive State**   

While LD-089 is actively cooperating with its host, the host becomes extraordinarily difficult to injure.    

Bullets, blunt trauma, falls, and most conventional weapons fail to produce meaningful damage.    

The entity appears to redistribute incoming damage throughout the paper structure surrounding the host.    

Researchers initially classified the host as effectively invulnerable.    

This assessment was later proven incorrect.    

The greatest danger to the host is LD-089 itself.    

**Loss of Control**   

LD-089 requires the host to maintain a conscious desire to control the entity.    

If the host becomes mentally exhausted, loses consciousness, voluntarily relinquishes control, or dies, LD-089 begins consuming them.    

The transformation is extremely disturbing.   

The host's body gradually loses its conventional biological structure and begins converting into anomalous paper.   

Skin becomes layered sheets.    
 
Hair becomes shredded strips of printed material.    

Muscle tissue becomes densely packed paper fibers.   

The host's physical form eventually collapses into an enormous humanoid mass of paper, posters, distorted photographs, and burning fragments.  

At this point, LD-089 has completely taken control.    

The original host is considered unrecoverable.    

**"The Distortion"**    

This fully manifested state is designated LD-089-1.    

LD-089-1 resembles a gigantic humanoid constructed from thousands of overlapping missing-person posters.    

Its proportions constantly change.    

Arms may extend several meters before folding back into the body.    

Wings can separate into enormous clouds of paper.    

Faces appear across its surface for fractions of a second before becoming unreadable again.    

The creature possesses the host's original voice, although it is frequently accompanied by hundreds of overlapping whispers.    

It retains access to the Paperbrand.    

Its combat capability dramatically exceeds that of the normal host state.    

Seven-Minute Limitation    

LD-089-1 cannot maintain physical existence indefinitely.    

Once the transformation occurs, the biological remnants of the host's brain begin shutting down.    
 
When not interfered with the maximum manifestation time is 7 minutes    

At approximately seven minutes, the entire structure begins collapsing.    

The paper loses cohesion.    

The wings disintegrate.    

The Paperbrand extinguishes.    

The remaining material folds inward and eventually becomes a single ordinary missing-person poster.    

The host does not return.    

The poster created afterward depicts the host as well of being laid under the original poster of LD-089.    

Their face is completely obscured.    

The Most Disturbing Discovery    

Researchers initially believed LD-089 was simply transforming its victims into paper.    
 
Further investigation revealed something considerably worse.    

The faces displayed on the posters aren't random.   

Every photograph appears to correspond to someone who has disappeared after coming into contact with LD-089.    

Some photographs belong to people missing for decades.    

Others belong to individuals who have disappeared only hours before the photograph appeared.   

At least one photograph has been identified as belonging to an individual who has not disappeared yet.    

The individual remains alive.    

This photo is to be assumed to be of ΕΤΔ (ETD) this assumption was reached after LD-089 stated after being questioned on the identity:     

“You know him. You have some of his stuff I won’t confirm who he is what are you going to do bring me to [redacted] just like UTC.”   

Psychological Effect    

Hosts frequently become obsessed with finding the identities represented on their wings.    

LD-089 refuses to answer.    
 
When questioned about the photographs, it repeatedly responds:   

"You already know them."    

When asked how:   

"You just haven't met them yet."    

Containment Incident 089-04   

A host maintained control of LD-089 for approximately eleven months.    

During this period, the host became one of DIVIDE's most effective anomalous-response assets.    

The host willingly activated the entity during high-risk operations and demonstrated exceptional combat capability.    

However, during a prolonged engagement, the host became unconscious.    

LD-089 immediately began taking control.    

The transformation lasted approximately six minutes and forty-one seconds before being stopped.   

The resulting entity breached three reinforced containment barriers before being neutralized.    

After its destruction, personnel recovered two pieces of paper.    

One displayed the host's missing-person photograph.    

The other seemed to be the original paper obtained by D.I.V.I.D.E.   

The date beneath the photograph read:    

[REDACTED]    

The date was eleven months before the host had ever encountered LD-089.
