# **SPECIAL INDIVIDUAL FILE — DR. MANARINE HYPERTHEOS**           

**Designation:** Special Individual — S.I.-001             
**Name:** Dr. Manarine Hypertheos           
**Known Titles:** “The Doctor Trying To Be God”           
**Occupation:** Former Junior Researcher / Senior Anomalous Correlation Researcher              
**Clearance:** DIVIDE Level 7+          
**Status:** Protected / Under Permanent Surveillance              
**Threat Classification:** **UNDETERMINED**             

---            

# **Overview**          

Dr. Manarine Hypertheos is a former D.I.V.I.D.E. researcher who entered the organization as a relatively inexperienced junior scientist.         

He initially specialized in anomalous behavioral patterns and cross-file comparison.            

Within several years, however, Dr. Hypertheos became responsible for identifying one of the largest anomalous correlations ever discovered.              

His research connected several apparently unrelated LDs to a single theoretical source.            

His first major discovery involved **LD-069** and **LD-088**.             

Dr. Hypertheos proposed that neither entity represented an independent anomaly.         

Instead, both appeared to possess characteristics consistent with being **derivative creations of an Omega-tier anomaly**.                       

The theory was dismissed by nearly every senior researcher.                       

At the time, D.I.V.I.D.E. had only confirmed the existence of a single Omega-tier anomaly:            

### **D**          

Dr. Hypertheos argued that D.I.V.I.D.E. had been making the wrong assumption.                    

He believed D was not necessarily unique.                    

He believed there was another.                    

---                    

# **Rise Through D.I.V.I.D.E.**                        

Following the submission of his research, Dr. Hypertheos rapidly advanced through the research hierarchy.                      

His discoveries included several previously ignored similarities between anomalies:                    

* Repeated appearances of the **ΕΤΔ** designation.                        
* Anomalies displaying abilities that appeared conceptually related rather than biologically related.               
* Contradictory records concerning the origins of multiple LDs.           
* Instances where an anomaly appeared to possess knowledge of entities it should never have encountered.                   
* Repeated references to concepts such as Life, Death, creation, destruction and reincarnation.                           

Hypertheos eventually proposed the following:                           
                   
> **“We are not cataloguing isolated anomalies.”**                      

> **“We are cataloguing fragments.”**                         

This theory became the foundation of what is now known as:                     

### **The Omega Correlation Hypothesis**                        

---                 

# **The Omega Correlation Hypothesis**                      

Dr. Hypertheos theorized that Omega-tier anomalies are fundamentally different from conventional LDs.                     

Rather than functioning as individual creatures or objects, Omega-tier anomalies may possess the ability to create, divide, influence, or manifest additional anomalies.                         

Under this theory:                           

**D** represents one Omega-tier entity.                     

A second Omega-tier entity may exist.                                

And LD-069, LD-088, and several other apparently unrelated anomalies may be fragments, creations, servants, or indirect consequences of that entity's existence.                    

Hypertheos initially estimated the number of Omega-tier entities to be:                     

**Two.**             

His reasoning was considered absurd.                    

Until Incident OH-088.                 

---                    

# **Incident OH-088 — “The Question”**                      

An **AT-Class personnel** was authorized to interact with LD-088 under controlled conditions.                     

The purpose of the interaction was to determine whether LD-088 possessed knowledge concerning Omega-tier anomalies.                       

During the questioning, the AT-Class subject asked:                           
                   
> **“How many Omega-tier anomalies exist?”**                           

LD-088 smiled.                 

It responded:                   

> **“Two.”**                      

The AT-Class subject asked what constituted an Omega-tier anomaly.                        

LD-088's smile disappeared.                        

For approximately eleven seconds, the entity remained completely silent.                          

It then asked:                   

> **“What do you offer?”**                       

The AT-Class personnel refused to provide a sacrifice.                     

LD-088 did not react immediately.                                         

Dr. Hypertheos, observing from behind one-way glass, reportedly ordered the questioning to continue under the threat of his daughters skinning.                                

The AT-Class personnel again refused.                          

LD-088 then stated:                 

> **“Unfortunately, not enough.”**                 

The anomaly subsequently drained the entirety of the individual's life force.                     

The subject died immediately.                 

LD-088 then turned toward the observation window.                   

It appeared to look directly at Dr. Hypertheos.                  

The anomaly moved toward the glass.                       

Its head passed through the one-way observation window without breaking the material.                   

At approximately this point, every audio recording within the chamber simultaneously failed.                 

No alarms.               

No shouting.                      

No ventilation systems.                  

No movement.                   

Nothing.                      

Dr. Hypertheos later stated that the silence itself was more disturbing than anything LD-088 had done.                 

LD-088 then spoke directly to him:                 

> **“Watch what you seek, Doctor.”**                  

Its head withdrew through the glass.                  

All sound returned simultaneously.

# **Aftermath**                 

Dr. Hypertheos changed dramatically following Incident OH-088.                   

His previous research was abandoned.                  

He began studying:                      
 
* D            
* LD-069                
* LD-088                 
* LD-089                 
* LD-090                 
* LD-091                
* LD-092                   
* LD-095                

and every anomaly displaying references to **ΕΤΔ**, Omega entities, creation, or concepts associated with Life and Death.               

The doctor began constructing increasingly elaborate correlation maps.                  

The maps eventually covered entire laboratory walls.                 

Connections were drawn between individual LDs, historical events, anomalous pulses, unexplained disappearances, and recovered artifacts.             

Several researchers described the resulting diagrams as:               

> **“A family tree for something that shouldn't have a family.”**                     

---              

# **The Manarine Hypothesis**                

Dr. Hypertheos eventually developed a theory that the second Omega-tier anomaly does not simply **control** its associated LDs.                  

He believes the entity may have **created them intentionally.**                

Under his current hypothesis:                   

LD-069 may represent one conceptual aspect.            

LD-088 may represent another.                   

Other anomalies associated with the **ΕΤΔ** designation may represent additional fragments.                 

The exact purpose of these creations remains unknown.              

Dr. Hypertheos believes their existence may be part of a larger process.                 

He has referred to this process only once:                  

> **“Reconstitution.”**                 

No further explanation has been given.               

---                

# **Current Research**                 

D.I.V.I.D.E. discovered that Dr. Hypertheos had begun experimenting with his own biology.                

The experiments were initially believed to involve anomalous resistance testing.                  

Further investigation revealed that the doctor was attempting to determine whether a human body could be **modified into a vessel capable of sustaining anomalous properties beyond human limits.**              

Several unauthorized procedures were discovered.             

The doctor had implanted anomalous materials into his own body.             

All removed samples were immediately confiscated.                

When questioned about his actions, Hypertheos responded:                

> **“I don't want power.”**                

The interviewer asked:               

> “Then what are you trying to accomplish?”                 

Dr. Hypertheos answered:                

> **“Understanding.”**               

He refused to elaborate.             

---             

# **Potential Objective**                            

D.I.V.I.D.E. currently suspects that Dr. Hypertheos may be attempting to reproduce the process by which the Omega-tier anomaly created or divided its power.                         

Whether this means he intends to:                    

* Become an anomaly.                  
* Create an anomaly.                      
* Reconstruct an Omega-tier entity.                          
* Reunite existing fragments.                            
* Or become something entirely different remains unknown.                         

His research increasingly suggests the possibility that he believes **humanity can artificially reach the same state as an Omega-tier entity.**                           

No proof has been found.

# **Protection Status**                   

Despite repeated concerns regarding his mental state and research practices, D.I.V.I.D.E. has elected not to terminate or permanently detain Dr. Hypertheos.                        

The reason is simple:                      

### He may be the single person with the greatest understanding of the Omega phenomenon.                       

If the doctor's research is correct, removing him could permanently eliminate D.I.V.I.D.E.'s best chance of understanding the second Omega-tier entity.                        

As a result, Dr. Hypertheos remains under:                         
                   
* 24-hour surveillance.                     
* Restricted laboratory access.                      
* Mandatory psychological evaluation.                      
* Restricted access to high-level anomalies.                       
* Continuous biometric monitoring.                      

He is prohibited from conducting unauthorized human experimentation.
