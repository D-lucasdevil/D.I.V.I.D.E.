Unlike most high-risk anomalies, LD-066 displays calm, polite, and cooperative behavior. Researchers universally agree that this demeanor is the only reason the anomaly remains under containment.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="redacted-stat">
      <div class="redacted-val">REDACTED</div>
      <div class="redacted-sub">Current threat classification — complete inability to establish upper capability limits</div>
    </div>

    <!-- PHYSICAL -->
    <div class="section-label">// Physical Description //</div>
    <div class="content-block">
      <p>LD-066 appears biologically human. Medical scans reveal no detectable internal organs, no measurable heartbeat, no respiration, and no signs of aging. Despite this, the anomaly remains fully functional.</p>
      <p>Attempts to collect biological samples have failed. Any instrument attempting to remove tissue from the entity is severed before contact is achieved. Researchers remain uncertain whether LD-066 possesses a true physical body.</p>
    </div>

    <!-- BEHAVIOR -->
    <div class="section-label">// Behavior //</div>
    <div class="content-block">
      <p>LD-066 is generally cooperative and respectful toward facility personnel. The anomaly frequently engages in conversation and willingly answers questions. However, it refuses to discuss its origin, purpose, the nature of its abilities, or the identity of its targets. When questioned regarding these topics, the entity commonly responds:</p>
    </div>
    <div class="latin-box">
      <div class="latin-original">"Non est vestrum scire."</div>
      <div class="latin-translation">"It is not yours to know."</div>
    </div>
    <div class="content-block">
      <p>The anomaly has never displayed unprovoked hostility. However, all attempts at forceful containment or termination have ended catastrophically.</p>
    </div>

      <p>LD-066 can summon anomalous scissors of varying size and composition. The scissors may appear in open air, underground, within structures, or directly adjacent to targets. The largest confirmed manifestation bisected an entire mountain ridge during a containment incident. Material analysis has proven impossible. All manifested scissors disappear shortly after use.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="list-item"><span class="arrow">▶</span>Complete bisection — applied to personnel, vehicles, and structures</div>

    <div class="section-label">// Spatial Cutting //</div>
    <div class="content-block">
      <p>LD-066 appears capable of cutting more than physical matter. Documented examples include severing containment barriers, splitting reinforced bunkers, dividing moving vehicles, and creating temporary openings through solid structures. Researchers believe the anomaly is not cutting objects themselves but rather the space occupied by those objects. This theory remains unconfirmed.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="section-label">// Immortality //</div>
    <div class="content-block">
      <p>No successful method of harming LD-066 has been discovered. Observed attempts include firearms, explosives, anti-anomaly weaponry, and reality destabilization devices. All efforts failed. The anomaly either avoided damage entirely or was completely left unaffected. Researchers currently consider LD-066 effectively immortal.</p>
    </div>

        <p>The resulting shockwave <strong>killed four security personnel</strong>, destroyed nearby equipment, and shattered reinforced glass panels. LD-066 remained in the bowed position until removed from the area. When questioned regarding the incident, she responded:</p>
