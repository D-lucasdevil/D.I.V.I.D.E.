# LD-088 — **"The Mercy of Life"**   

**Designation:** Existential Anomaly    
**Threat Level:** **REDACTED**   
**Clearance Required:** DIVIDE Level 6+   
**Containment Status:** **NON-CONTAINABLE**   

> **NOTICE:** LD-088 is not to be summoned, contacted, worshipped, or intentionally sought out.   
> Personnel expressing an overwhelming desire to encounter LD-088 are to be terminated      

---   
 
## Overview    

LD-088 manifests as a humanoid entity resembling an enormous winged angel.     

Its appearance varies slightly between encounters, but all manifestations possess multiple wings, several arms, and an almost unnaturally peaceful expression.   

LD-088 does not appear hostile.    

It does not hunt.    

It does not attack.    
  
It does not force itself upon unwilling individuals.      

Instead, LD-088 appears exclusively to individuals experiencing an **extreme and immediate desire to remain alive.**    

Terminally ill patients.    

People trapped in disasters.    

Individuals moments away from death.    

Those facing otherwise certain fatal circumstances.   

The entity will appear without warning.    

All surrounding sound ceases during its manifestation.    

Fire produces no noise.     

Machines stop.    

People become unable to hear their own voices.    

LD-088 then speaks directly to the individual.    

Its voice has consistently been described as **"the warmest voice I have ever heard."**      

This description is universal.    

And universally followed by fear.    

---    

# The Offer    

LD-088 asks only one question:    

> **"Do you wish to live?"**     

If the individual refuses, LD-088 disappears.     

If they accept, LD-088 asks:     

> **"What will you give me?"**    

The individual must personally state what they are willing to sacrifice.    

LD-088 never specifies what it wants.     

It allows the individual to decide.     

Anything can theoretically be offered.    

Their possessions.    

Their future.    

Their relationships.    

Their happiness.    

Their memories.    

Their body.    

Their soul.    

Their entire existence.    

The individual may even say:    

> **"Anything."**    

LD-088 accepts.   

The moment the agreement is made, the individual's survival becomes **an absolute condition of reality.**    

---    

# The Price    

LD-088 does not necessarily take the thing the individual *thought* they were offering.    

Instead, reality begins interpreting their request literally.     

Someone who says:    

**"I'll give anything to live."**     

may discover that nothing is permitted to kill them.     

Not disease.    

Not injury.    

Not accidents.    

Not old age.    

Not their own actions.    

Their survival becomes more important than their wellbeing.     

And LD-088 never considers this a punishment.    

It considers it **fulfillment of the contract.**    

---    

## Secondary Effect — "The Preservation"    

Those who receive LD-088's blessing frequently experience increasingly abnormal circumstances surrounding their continued existence.     

Common effects include:    

* Severe chronic pain with no identifiable cause.    
* Loved ones dying through unrelated circumstances.    
* Relationships gradually collapsing.    
* The subject becoming increasingly isolated.    
* Attempts to escape their circumstances failing through improbable coincidences.    
* Medical conditions becoming impossible to terminate despite conventional treatment.    
* Situations that should result in death instead producing survival with permanent consequences.    

The anomaly does not appear to directly cause every event.    

Rather, reality itself appears to **remove anything capable of ending the subject's life.**      

Researchers therefore suspect LD-088 does not actually *grant immortality.*     

It simply prevents the universe from allowing the individual to die.     

---    

# The Worst Part    

LD-088 can return.    

If the subject later begs it to reverse the agreement, the entity simply reminds them:     

> **"You wanted to live."**     

If they attempt to bargain again, LD-088 responds:    

> **"You already gave me everything."**    

No second bargain has ever been accepted.     

---    

# Incident LD-088-01    

The first extensively documented encounter involved a sixteen-year-old individual involved in a catastrophic vehicular accident.    

The subject was believed to have minutes remaining.     

Witnesses reported hearing the victim begging repeatedly for their life.      

Then everything stopped.    

No traffic.    

No sirens.   

No screaming.     

No fire.    

No wind.    

LD-088 appeared.   

The entity approached the victim and asked:    

> **"Do you wish to live?"**    

The subject answered yes.    

When asked what they would give in exchange, the subject reportedly answered:     

> **"Anything. Anything you want. Anything I have or don't have."**     

LD-088 laughed.     

It then said:     

> **"Then you shall live."**    

The subject survived.     

Doctors initially considered the incident miraculous.     

Over the following years, however, an extremely unusual pattern emerged.      

People who developed close relationships with the subject began experiencing severe psychological disturbances.      

Several relationships ended catastrophically ussualy suicides of people around him that were so graphic even we thought it was the entity it self doing it to them no it was them selfs.      

The subject became increasingly isolated.           

At twenty-two, after years of deterioration, the individual reportedly begged for death and attempted to terminate the agreement.    

LD-088 manifested again.    

The subject asked it to take back the gift.     

LD-088 responded:      

> **"Didn't you want to live?"**     

The subject begged for mercy.     

The entity answered:      

> **"I did what you asked of me."**      

When the subject reminded LD-088 that they had offered everything, the entity reportedly smiled.     

> **"Have you forgotten?"**       
> **"You already offered me everything."**      
> **"You have nothing left to give."**      

LD-088 disappeared.     

The subject subsequently remained alive for decades despite numerous circumstances that should have been fatal.      

Eventually, at approximately one hundred years of age, the subject died from natural causes.       

**LD-088 did not manifest.**

# DIVIDE Theoretical Classification     

LD-088 presents an unprecedented classification problem.     

It has demonstrated no conventional combat capability.    

It has never attacked DIVIDE personnel.     

It has never demonstrated hostility toward humanity.     

It may even be possible to classify its actions as **benevolent** under an extremely literal interpretation.     

Nevertheless, Level 6 researchers have unanimously rejected assigning it a conventional Tier.    

One researcher summarized the problem:     

> **"You can't put a threat level on something that doesn't need to fight you."**
