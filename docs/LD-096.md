# **LD-096 — “The Candle Maiden”**    

**Designation:** Anomalous Humanoid / Beneficial Entity     
**Threat Level:** **Class II — Tier 2**     
**Clearance Required:** D.I.V.I.D.E. Level 5+   
**Containment Status:** Contained — Cooperative     
**Current Status:** Stable

# **Overview**     

LD-096 is a humanoid female entity resembling a young woman dressed in traditional dark clothing.      

The entity possesses extremely pale skin, dark hair, and permanently closed eyes covered by what appears to be **solidified candle wax**.     

The wax does not appear to be physically attached to the entity's eyelids. Attempts to remove it have consistently failed, as the wax immediately reforms.     

Despite being apparently unable to see, LD-096 demonstrates complete awareness of its surroundings.       

The entity is consistently calm and displays a gentle, almost melancholic personality.     

LD-096 has shown no natural tendency toward violence.     

It frequently spends its time sitting quietly within its containment area, praying, sleeping, or interacting with D.I.V.I.D.E. personnel who voluntarily approach it.       

LD-096 has repeatedly referred to humans as:      

> **“The people I was meant to save.”**          

---       

# **Primary Anomalous Property — The Blessing**         

LD-096 is capable of granting a temporary anomalous blessing to a living individual.         

The blessing is most commonly administered through physical contact, with LD-096 generally embracing the recipient.       

During the process, the entity emits a bright **yellow-golden light**.        

The recipient reports feeling an extreme sense of warmth, calmness, and safety.        

The blessing appears to bind LD-096's anomalous properties to the recipient.          

Once blessed, the individual becomes capable of being **revived by LD-096 after death.**            

The revival does not require LD-096 to be physically present at the location of death.          

When a blessed individual dies, their body disappears and reappears near LD-096 after an unknown period of time.          

The exact mechanics behind this process remain unknown.          

---         

# **Limitations of the Blessing**         

LD-096 cannot resurrect an individual indefinitely.         

Current testing indicates that a single person can survive a maximum of approximately **five revivals**.          

Every subsequent revival causes progressively greater deterioration.         

The deterioration appears to affect both the body and the individual's anomalous stability.             

### **First Revival**      

Generally produces no permanent physical changes.         

The individual may experience:            

* extreme exhaustion           
* temporary confusion         
* intense fear         
* vivid memories of their death         

### **Second Revival**        

Minor physical abnormalities begin appearing.             

These may include:           

* abnormal muscle growth           
* discoloration of the skin                
* changes in the eyes            
* increased aggression            
* involuntary tremors             

### **Third Revival**            

The individual's body begins undergoing significant transformation.            

Subjects may develop:           

* enlarged limbs        
* abnormal bone growth            
* distorted hands          
* sharpened teeth           
* increased physical strength               

Psychological deterioration also becomes significantly more apparent.          

### **Fourth Revival**          
 
The subject begins displaying increasingly animalistic behavior.            

Communication becomes difficult.         

The individual may experience:           

* uncontrollable rage            
* extreme paranoia           
* loss of empathy         
* compulsive aggression           
* distorted vocalizations           

### **Fifth Revival**             

The individual is considered **critically unstable**.       

The human body can no longer properly contain the accumulated anomalous influence.            

Death during this stage is expected to result in complete transformation into **LD-096-1.**

# **LD-096-1 — “The Despair Beast”**           

LD-096-1 is the result of a blessed individual exceeding the safe limits of LD-096's resurrection ability.           

The resulting entity bears little resemblance to its original human form.          

The body becomes heavily distorted, with enlarged limbs, abnormal skeletal structures, excessive muscle growth, and other mutations unique to the individual.           

No two instances of LD-096-1 are expected to look identical.

The transformation appears to be influenced by the individual's accumulated psychological suffering and experiences.           

For this reason, D.I.V.I.D.E. researchers have unofficially designated the resulting entities as:           

> **“Beasts of Despair.”**              

LD-096-1 instances retain fragments of the original individual's memories but are generally incapable of rational communication.          

Their primary behavior is to attack and kill living organisms in their surroundings.        

**LD-096 itself is universally ignored by LD-096-1.**         

The entity will not attack its creator under normal circumstances.        

LD-096-1 remains classified as **Tier II** because the resulting creatures, despite their extreme aggression, are physically vulnerable.         

Standard firearms have proven effective against confirmed instances.            

---         

# **Healing Ability**             

LD-096 possesses a limited healing ability separate from its resurrection blessing.     

Through direct contact, LD-096 can accelerate the natural healing process of living organisms.          

Minor wounds can be healed within seconds.            

More severe injuries require significantly longer contact.            

The ability cannot:         

* regenerate completely destroyed organs             
* reverse advanced disease            
* restore a completely destroyed brain            
* resurrect the dead without the Blessing            
* repair severe anomalous corruption                 

The entity appears to deliberately limit its healing ability.           

When asked why, LD-096 reportedly answered:              

> **“Some things must be allowed to end.”**          

---             

# **LD-096's Own Durability**              

Despite possessing anomalous abilities capable of resurrecting others, LD-096 itself is extraordinarily fragile.             

The entity possesses approximately human-level physical durability.             

A knife, firearm, blunt-force trauma, or even sufficiently powerful physical assault can kill LD-096.                

This vulnerability appears to be intentional rather than accidental.             

When killed, LD-096 does not remain dead.          

The entity eventually reappears at an unknown location somewhere on Earth.             

The location appears to be completely random.             

D.I.V.I.D.E. has been unable to determine whether LD-096 chooses its new location or whether the process is automatic.              

---             

# **The Seventh Death**           

LD-096 has been successfully killed multiple times during controlled testing.          

However, researchers have established a strict prohibition against allowing LD-096 to die **seven times.**          

The consequences of a seventh death remain unknown.          

LD-096 itself becomes visibly distressed whenever the subject is mentioned.          

When asked what would happen after its seventh death, the entity remained silent for approximately seventeen seconds before responding:           

> **“I don't know.”**         

When asked whether the world would be safe, LD-096 responded:             

> **“No.”**         

No further explanation was provided.         

D.I.V.I.D.E. researchers currently theorize that the seventh death would cause a fundamental change in LD-096's anomalous nature.          

Several theoretical models predict the resulting entity could potentially reach **Tier V classification.**          

This remains entirely speculative.             

**Under no circumstances is LD-096 to be intentionally killed seven times.**

# **Containment Procedure**           

LD-096 is housed within a comfortable humanoid containment chamber rather than a conventional reinforced cell.            

The chamber contains:          

* sleeping quarters             
* furniture              
* books          
* religious and philosophical texts         
* artificial windows            
* controlled lighting             
* recreational materials                

LD-096 does not require food, water, or conventional biological maintenance.              

Personnel are permitted to interact with the entity provided they possess appropriate clearance.              

Personnel are prohibited from:            

* intentionally harming LD-096        
* testing the seventh-death hypothesis          
* repeatedly requesting blessings          
* intentionally exposing personnel to LD-096-1           
* attempting to remove the wax covering its eyes          

Individuals seeking a blessing must receive authorization from a Level 4 researcher.            

Blessings are not to be used as a substitute for conventional medical treatment or personnel replacement.

# **Incident 096-B**          

During a containment breach involving multiple hostile anomalies, a D.I.V.I.D.E. BESERK operator was killed by a confirmed **Tier IV anomaly**.           

The operator had previously received LD-096's blessing.           

Approximately several minutes after his death, the operator reappeared directly inside LD-096's containment chamber.        

The individual was alive.          

LD-096 immediately approached him.           

Instead of expressing relief, the operator began screaming.              

> **“MONSTER! DEMON!”**           

The operator attempted to retreat from LD-096 despite displaying no physical injuries.           

LD-096 appeared confused and visibly distressed.          

The operator was later cleared for active duty following psychological evaluation.           

---          

# **Incident 096-C**             

The same BESERK operator was later killed during another operation.          

LD-096 successfully resurrected him for a second time.             

The resurrection initially appeared successful.           

Approximately thirty seconds after revival, however, the operator's body began undergoing rapid anomalous deformation.           

The subject's limbs distorted.             

The skeletal structure expanded.          

Muscle tissue rapidly increased in mass.           

The subject began screaming before completely losing the ability to communicate.            

The resulting entity was designated **LD-096-1.**         

The creature immediately attacked nearby personnel.            

Three BESERK operators engaged the entity.          

LD-096-1 was eliminated after sustained small-arms fire.           

No personnel fatalities occurred.             

---         

# **Researcher Observation**          

Several hours after the incident, Researcher **Dr. Connor** encountered LD-096 walking alone through a D.I.V.I.D.E. corridor.          

The entity was crying.          

When asked what was wrong, LD-096 initially refused to respond.          

After several minutes, it began quietly repeating:          

> **“Why does this always happen?”**            

Dr. Connor asked what it meant.          

LD-096 screamed:          

> **“I just want the best for you.”**             

The entity then covered its face with its hands.          

> **“I don't want all of you to die.”**             

LD-096 subsequently returned to its containment chamber without further explanation.
