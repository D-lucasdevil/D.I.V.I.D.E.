# **LD-097 — “The Cannibalistic Meat Cleaver”**           

**Designation:** Anomalous Weapon / Biological Artifact                 
**Threat Level:** **Class II — Tier 2**                  
**Clearance Required:** DIVIDE Level 4+                
**Containment Status:** **UNCONTAINED**                  
**Current Location:** UNKNOWN                   
**Current Threat Status:** ACTIVE / MISSING                   

---                          
                
# **Overview**                           

LD-097, designated **“The Cannibalistic Meat Cleaver,”** is an anomalous butcher's cleaver composed entirely of unidentified biological material.                       

The handle appears to consist of fused bone, cartilage, and dense muscle tissue, while the blade itself appears to have been formed from an extremely dense and unusually sharp bone-like substance.                     

Despite its organic appearance, LD-097 does not decay, produce biological waste, or display any conventional signs of metabolism.                 

The weapon is extraordinarily durable and appears to become progressively stronger through the **consumption of human flesh by its wielder**.                   

Unlike several other anomalous weapons, LD-097 possesses **no visible counter, display, meter, or other method of determining its accumulated strength.**                         

Researchers therefore have no reliable method of determining how powerful LD-097 has become at any given time.                         

---               

# **Anomalous Influence**                          
                   
Upon voluntarily picking up LD-097, the wielder experiences an immediate psychological shift.                   

The individual develops an intense desire to consume **human flesh**.                  

Animal meat, synthetic food, and other biological materials do not satisfy the compulsion and produce no observed anomalous reaction.                  

The effect becomes increasingly severe the longer LD-097 remains in the individual's possession.                    

Early symptoms include:                     

* Increased hunger.                      
* Intrusive thoughts concerning human flesh.                   
* Irritability.                  
* Fixation on human scent.                   
* Increasing difficulty resisting the compulsion.                        

Extended exposure produces increasingly severe symptoms.            

Eventually, the wielder begins actively seeking opportunities to obtain human flesh.                   

The psychological effect does not appear to constitute direct mind control.                 

Individuals retain the ability to make decisions.                    

However, resisting LD-097 becomes progressively more difficult.                  

---                        

# **Strength Amplification**                         

LD-097 becomes stronger whenever its wielder successfully consumes **human tissue**.                   

The exact relationship between consumption and increased weapon strength remains unknown.                  

Current evidence suggests:                    

**More human flesh consumed = greater LD-097 output.**                    

Only human tissue appears capable of feeding the weapon's anomalous properties.                    

The cleaver itself shows no obvious physical change as its strength increases.                   

There is therefore **no confirmed upper limit** to its potential strength.                    

---                   

# **Observed Effects**                    

At low levels of activity, LD-097 is already capable of functioning as an extremely effective cutting weapon.            

After prolonged feeding, recorded capabilities have included:               

* Cutting heavy wooden doors.                 
* Severing reinforced security equipment.                
* Damaging armored personnel equipment.                
* Cutting through structural concrete.                
* Splitting reinforced walls.                          

The maximum confirmed output remains unknown.                     

---                         
              
# **Incident 097-03**                        

During an authorized anomalous stress test, an AT-Class subject was permitted to interact with LD-097.              
                
The subject subsequently consumed approximately **18 human bodies** over the course of the experiment.                   

Following the experiment, the subject was ordered to perform a controlled test against a reinforced concrete wall.                  

The subject struck the wall once.            

LD-097 passed through the structure with almost no measurable resistance.              

The wall was divided nearly perfectly along the strike path.               

The weapon showed no visible damage.                

No equivalent stress test has been authorized since.                 

This remains the **strongest recorded output of LD-097.**

# **Loss of Containment**                      
               
LD-097 was previously stored within a DIVIDE anomalous weapons facility.                        

Its containment status changed following an unauthorized breach.                      

At approximately 03:17 local time, an unidentified individual entered the facility.                    

Security footage captured a male individual approximately:                  

**Height:** ~173 cm            
**Hair:** Short brown                
**Build:** Average                     

The individual's face could not be reliably identified.                

No usable fingerprints were recovered.                  

The individual successfully reached the anomalous weapons storage area and removed LD-097.              

The thief escaped the facility before response personnel were able to intercept them.

Several months later, D.I.V.I.D.E. intelligence identified LD-097 at an underground anomalous auction.                 

The weapon reportedly sold for approximately:                 

## **$120,000,000 USD**                   

The buyer's identity remains unknown.                            

Available intelligence strongly suggests the purchaser may be connected to an anomalous extremist organization known for worshipping predatory LDs.             

Several intercepted communications contain references to:                    

> **“The Hungry Blade.”**                

and                 

> **“The Feast That Never Ends.”**                         

No direct confirmation has been obtained that this group currently possesses LD-097.                        

---
                    
# **Current Status**                                

LD-097 has not been recovered.                         

Because the weapon's strength depends upon the consumption of human flesh, D.I.V.I.D.E. considers the artifact an **active escalating threat.**                

A wielder who obtains LD-097 and repeatedly feeds its anomalous properties may eventually create a weapon significantly more dangerous than the one originally contained by DIVIDE.                    

Unlike contained anomalous weapons, LD-097 cannot currently be monitored.                

Its current accumulated strength is therefore:                 

> **UNKNOWN.**              

---                   

# **Psychological Progression**                  

D.I.V.I.D.E. researchers have identified four general stages of LD-097 influence.                

### **Stage I — Craving**                   

The wielder begins experiencing intrusive thoughts concerning human flesh.                   

### **Stage II — Consumption**                  

The wielder develops a strong compulsion to obtain and consume human tissue.                   

Attempts to substitute other food sources provide no relief.                 

### **Stage III — Obsession**                       

The wielder becomes increasingly preoccupied with finding human prey.                 

Normal eating behavior gradually becomes secondary to the anomalous compulsion.                       

### **Stage IV — Feast**                

The wielder becomes almost completely dominated by the desire to consume human flesh.                    

At this stage, recovery is considered extremely unlikely.                 

---                  

# **Weaknesses**                      

Despite its increasing offensive capability, LD-097 possesses several major limitations.                      

The weapon itself cannot:             

* Move independently.               
* Attack without a wielder.               
* Control someone who refuses to touch it.              
* Generate human tissue.               
* Increase its power without being fed.                 
* Gain strength from animal tissue or non-human biological material.                

The wielder is therefore the primary point of failure.                     

If the individual can be separated from LD-097 before extensive feeding occurs, termination of the anomalous threat becomes significantly easier.                      

---               

# **Containment Directive**                   

All D.I.V.I.D.E. personnel are instructed:                  

**DO NOT TOUCH LD-097.**                   

If recovered, personnel are forbidden from testing the weapon's maximum potential.                   

The artifact is to be transported using automated containment equipment and returned directly to high-security anomalous storage.                    

Under no circumstances is LD-097 to be intentionally fed.              

---                

# **Threat Assessment**               

LD-097 remains classified as:              

## **CLASS II — TIER 2**                        

The weapon itself does not possess sufficient autonomous capabilities to justify a higher classification.                 

Its greatest danger is its **scaling potential**.                        

A small group of determined individuals could theoretically transform LD-097 into an increasingly powerful anomalous weapon given enough time and access to human victims.             

If intelligence confirms that LD-097 has been deliberately fed on a large scale, **immediate reassessment of its threat classification is mandatory.**
